About the specialist

Technique, anatomy and refined taste in every detail.

I believe facial aesthetics is not about transforming faces, but about refining details. My work is to unite technique, planning and an aesthetic eye to create personalized results — respecting proportions, preserving individual features and always pursuing naturalness.

Every appointment starts with a detailed assessment — because a beautiful result is born from a personalized treatment plan, not from ready-made formulas.
